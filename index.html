Neon Dash - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Summary

Neon Dash does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

Data on your device

The game saves your best scores, selected difficulty and sound setting on your device only, so they are available next time you play. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

No accounts, ads or tracking

The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. It does not need an internet connection and does not request any device permissions.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
